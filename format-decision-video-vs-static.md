# Format Decision: Video vs. Static/Text Ads (2026)

Companion to `facebook-lead-gen-playbook.md`. Answers the question: **for a same-day Atlanta term-life launch under Meta's Insurance Special Ad Category, do video ads or static/text-image ads?**

---

## TL;DR — The honest answer

**For TODAY (5 PM launch): start with static + carousel. Layer video in on Day 2–3.**

This contradicts the original playbook's video-first emphasis. The 2026 data + your timeline forces this.

Reasoning in three lines:
1. **Production speed.** You can ship 10 static variations in the time it takes to produce one polished video. With Meta's SAC review window eating ~2–6 hours, every hour you save in production is an hour the campaign delivers.
2. **CPL economics.** 2026 industry data: single-image ads avg ~$38.60 CPL vs. video ~$45.80 CPL on Meta. For raw inbound volume — your Pillar 5 goal — static wins on first-touch cost.
3. **Static still drives 60–70% of Meta conversions** in 2026 despite the Reels-first narrative.

**But** — video produces higher-quality leads (better qualification rates, better trust for insurance specifically). The right long-term mix is hybrid: ~60% static for volume, video for retargeting and the trust layer that closes the age gap.

## Decision checklist — pick your format in <5 minutes

Score yourself on each row. Mostly LEFT → static-first today. Mostly RIGHT → video-first.

| Factor | Static / Text-Image | Video |
|---|---|---|
| **Time to launch** | ≤4 hours away | ≥1 day available |

| **Compliance review window** | Ad copy already pre-approved by Primerica | Compliance can review video same-day |

| **Creative iteration plan** | Want to test 5+ hook angles fast | Want to ride one strong story |

**Your situation (23M, Atlanta, same-day 5 PM, SAC = Insurance):** the top 4 rows tilt hard toward static-first. The bottom 4 tilt toward video being valuable but as a Day 2–3 layer, not Day 1.

## Static-first rollout (today, 5 PM target)

### 1. Build 5 static creatives in ~60 minutes

Format: **vertical 9:16, mobile-first, 1080×1920**. Tools: Canva (free), CapCut, or Figma. No fancy design — text-on-photo wins on Meta in 2026.

Five hooks, one image each (recycle the three Pillar 2 hooks + 2 new ones below):

1. **"If your paycheck stopped Friday, how long before your family felt it?"** — bold sans-serif white text on a dim photo of an Atlanta neighborhood / kitchen table.
2. **"The mortgage doesn't pause when you do."** — text over an exterior shot of a typical Atlanta home (your own, or a free-licensed stock photo).
3. **"23 and licensed. Quoting Atlanta families this week."** — selfie of you in the same wardrobe from the playbook (clean quarter-zip / button-down), shot at chest-up, caption text overlaid. *This is your trust ad.*
4. **"Term life for healthy 25–45 year olds usually costs less than your streaming bill."** — image of a remote/TV with a bill stack, text overlay. (No specific dollar amount — keeps you compliant.)
5. **"Free term life quote. 4 questions. 30 seconds. Atlanta only."** — pure typographic ad, brand-color background, big bold text. This is your "intent ad" — it filters for people ready to act.

**Critical text rules (2026 Meta):**
- Headline ≤27 characters (anything longer truncates on mobile feed)
- Primary text first sentence is what 90% of users see — front-load the avatar callout (Pillar 2 formulas)
- 6% text-on-image cap is no longer enforced as a hard ceiling, but ads with >50% text still get throttled in delivery — keep text to ~40% of the image area max

### 2. Carousel ad (do one, alongside the 5 statics)

A 4-card carousel often beats single images on lead-gen because it gives Meta multiple thumbnails to test:

- **Card 1:** "Atlanta parents — read this before your next mortgage payment." (image: house exterior)
- **Card 2:** "If something happened to you, the bills don't pause." (image: bill stack)
- **Card 3:** "Term life can replace your paycheck for your family." (image: parent + kid silhouette, stock OK)
- **Card 4:** "Tap below for a free quote — 30 seconds, no pressure." (image: phone with a form on screen / your headshot)

Each card has its own headline + description; the CTA on every card → same Instant Form.

### 3. Ship it

Drop the 5 static + 1 carousel into the same ad set as a single test wave. Same Instant Form, same speed-to-lead protocol. Let Meta auto-allocate. Kill bottom 2 after ~24h or 50 impressions per ad, whichever first.

### 4. Why text-only/typographic ads work in SAC

Because Meta blocks demographic targeting in Insurance SAC, the **literal text on the image is your targeting filter.** A 1080×1920 ad that just says "Atlanta parents — free term life quote" in a giant font is doing the audience selection your targeting can't. Don't underestimate plain-text ads in 2026 — they're cheap to produce, fast to iterate, and the algorithm reads them just like a video hook.

## Video layer (Day 2 onward)

Don't scrap the three Pillar 3 video scripts — but treat them as **Wave 2**, not Wave 1.

**Day 2 plan once Wave 1 is live:**
1. Shoot all 3 videos in one ~90-minute session (same outfit/setting/lighting for algorithmic continuity).
2. Add as **new ads in the same ad set**, NOT a new ad set. Lets Meta judge them against the static winners head-to-head with shared learning.
3. Compare CPL after 72 hours. Expected pattern: video CPL 15–25% higher, but lead-to-appointment conversion 20–40% better. If your appointment-conversion rate confirms that, shift budget toward video.
4. Once you have 100+ leads, build a **retargeting ad set** (Custom Audience: video viewers + Instant Form openers who didn't submit). Video is BUILT for this stage — trust + face-to-camera empathy is what closes the ambivalent ones.

**Don't skip video forever.** Insurance is a trust-purchase. The age-gap problem the playbook addresses (23 selling to 35-year-old parents) is solved by video, not static. Static wins on volume; video wins on close rate. You need both eventually.

## Reality check on "deep research" claims

A few things I want to be honest about:

- **Industry CPL benchmarks vary wildly by source.** $18–$45 is the most common 2026 range for life insurance, but I've seen reports as low as $9 (Medicare-adjacent leads) and as high as $80+ (high-intent annuity leads). Your actual CPL depends more on creative + speed-to-lead than on industry averages.
- **Video vs. static performance studies are usually ecom or SaaS.** Insurance-specific A/B data is thinner. The 60/40 split recommendation is a synthesis, not a hard rule for your vertical.
- **The 2026 Meta SAC rules can shift quietly.** Verify the current restrictions in Ads Manager itself (when you toggle SAC = Insurance, Meta tells you what's locked) rather than trusting any article — that's the source of truth.
- **Primerica compliance > everything in this doc.** If your RVP / Field Marketing team has a different opinion on video vs. static, follow theirs. They've watched their reps' ads succeed and fail in this exact market.

## Final answer to your question

**Are you "expected" to do video or static?**

You're not expected to do either. You're expected to ship leads. For today's 5 PM launch, static + carousel is the higher-EV move on raw volume and time-to-launch. Video is the right Wave 2 — it'll out-convert static on appointment booking once you have it produced and approved. Run both within the first 7 days; let the data, not the format, win.
